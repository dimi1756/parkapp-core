ParkApp by Urban Sync — Smart Parking & Mobility Platform

ParkApp is a crowdsourced, real-time parking platform. Drivers declare a spot the moment they leave it, or report one they spotted nearby, and nearby drivers can claim it and get live turn-by-turn navigation straight to it.

For drivers

Find and claim real, currently-available parking spots on a live map, earn points and badges for contributing accurate spot reports, and track your own parking history.

For municipalities

A live admin dashboard shows real-time spot occupancy, a citywide activity heatmap, daily/weekly traffic trends, and key parking KPIs for the whole municipality.
